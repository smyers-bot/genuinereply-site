Questions people actually ask

If yours isn't here, just ask — that's what Get Started is for.

Can't I just do this myself?

Yes — and most owners know they should reply to reviews, and mostly don't, because it's tedious and easy to put off. GenuineReply isn't replacing something you couldn't do; it's replacing something that consistently doesn't get done. That's the actual pitch: not "you can't," but "you don't, and now you don't have to think about it."

What if I don't like a reply it drafts?

Edit it before you approve it, every time — nothing posts without your review unless you've specifically turned on auto-send for positive reviews. Low-rated reviews always wait for you personally, no exceptions.

Is this just a generic chatbot bolted onto my reviews?

No. Drafts are built from examples of how you actually reply, and respond to the specific thing the customer said — the server they named, the dish that was cold, whatever it was — not a canned "thank you for your feedback." See real examples on the Examples page.

What happens with a really bad or complicated review?

Anything at or below your rating threshold (2 stars by default) is always held for manual approval — never auto-sent. You read it, you decide how to handle it, possibly off-platform entirely if it needs a phone call instead of a public reply.

Do I need to install anything or change how I use Google?

No new app for your customers, nothing changes about how reviews come in. GenuineReply connects to your existing Google Business Profile and works alongside it.

I run more than one location — does this work for a group?

Yes — pricing is per-location, and we'll work out a flat group rate for multi-location accounts. Tell us how many and we'll figure out what makes sense.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no long-term contract. We're early-stage and working closely with our first businesses, so if something isn't working for you, tell us — we'd genuinely rather fix it than have you leave quietly.

Are you only working with restaurants in South Carolina right now?

That's where we started — GenuineReply is being built hand-in-hand with a real multi-location restaurant group there — but the product isn't location-specific. If you're elsewhere and this sounds useful, reach out.
