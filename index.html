Get in touch

Reviews pile up. Replies don't.

Most businesses get reviews constantly and reply to almost none of them — not because they don't care, but because it's tedious and easy to put off. An unanswered review, good or bad, looks worse to anyone comparing you against a competitor who does reply.

How it works

1

A review comes in

Repline watches your Google Business Profile and picks up every new review automatically.

2

AI drafts a reply in your voice

Not a generic "thanks for your feedback" — a specific reply that responds to what the customer actually said, matched to how you talk.

3

You approve, or it sends itself

Review and edit with one click, or turn on auto-send for your positive reviews and only be pinged when something needs your attention.

Bad reviews are never auto-sent. Ever.

Anything at 1 or 2 stars is always held for you to read and approve personally — never sent automatically, no matter what. Repline drafts a thoughtful response ready for your review, but the decision on anything sensitive stays yours.
